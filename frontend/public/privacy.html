Demo information · October 14, 2026

Privacy, in this demo.

This page explains the fictional Welina analytics workspace. It is not a production patient-record service.

Do not enter real patient names, identifiers, or confidential clinical information. All supplied records are fictional.

What stays in your browser

The app uses localStorage to keep demo observations, annotations, target-phase changes, and your navigation preference on this device. The frontend does not send these edits to the .NET API or to an analytics service.

Storage is tied to this browser and site address. Other devices will not see your changes. If storage is blocked or full, the app warns you that changes may not persist.

How to remove demo records

Use Reset demo data in the workspace to restore the original fictional fixture. To remove all stored demo records and preferences, clear this site’s browser storage. Updating the fixture may also replace older demo records automatically.

Downloads

CSV, ZIP, SVG, and PNG exports are generated in your browser. You control the downloaded files. Clearing browser storage does not remove files you have already downloaded.

Cookies, fonts, and hosting

The app adds no tracking cookies and includes no advertising or analytics SDK. Fonts and application assets are served with the site, rather than requested from a third-party font provider.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address, under GitHub’s own policies. Following the project’s GitHub link leaves this demo.

Questions or problems

Report technical problems in the project’s issue tracker. Do not include confidential information in a public issue.
