DEMO LEARNING MATERIAL — Interval observation quick guide

Whole interval: score occurrence only if the response lasts throughout the
interval. Partial interval: score if it occurs at any point. Momentary time
sampling: score only at the observation instant. Log occurred and total
intervals separately, including zero-occurrence sessions. An unobserved
session is Data Not Tracked, not a zero.

Illustrative training content only; not a clinical prescription.
