DEMO LEARNING MATERIAL — Food labeling picture prompts

Use familiar picture cards (apple, banana, bread, carrot and yogurt).
Show one card at a time. Ask, "What is this?" and wait five seconds.
Follow the target's assigned prompt hierarchy. Record independent correct
responses and the number of trials. Rotate cards and practice with a second
therapist or caregiver when generalizing.

Illustrative training content only; not a clinical prescription.
